**Loops of Wonder**
Imagine a rabbit in a never-ending field, hopped across by you every hour, painting tiny little dots on a canvas as it goes. That's essentially what a loop is in programming - a way to repeat a set of actions over and over, like your every step leaving a mark on the field. You could say, "paint a dot at each hop" and the rabbit will do it a thousand times, creating a beautiful landscape.

**The Recursive Riddle**
In computer programs, we have something called recursion: a puzzle where the answer is the same as the question, but with smaller pieces. Think of a matryoshka doll - a doll inside a doll, each one a smaller version of the one before. When a program calls a function that does the same task, but smaller, it's recursion - a never-ending puzzle within a puzzle.
