**Unlocking Secrets with Triple Quotes**

Did you know that Python's triple quotes (`'''` or `"""`) are a superpower waiting to be unleashed? These mysterious marks can turn a line of code into a multiline string, or better yet, create a docstring that reveals the purpose and usage of your functions. By wrapping a docstring around your code, you'll make it crystal clear to anyone reading your code (including your future self!) what's happening behind the curtains. 

**Tip: Docstrings are your friend**

As you write functions in Python, remember to add a docstring to provide context and clarity. When you run `help()` on a function, Python will display its docstring, revealing its purpose, arguments, and return values. This helps other developers understand how to use your code and reduces the number of "how-do-I-use-this?" emails.
