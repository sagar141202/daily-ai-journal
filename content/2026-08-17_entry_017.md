**Morning Mind Dump**

Every morning, as the warm sunlight seeps through the blinds, I take a few moments to jot down my thoughts, ideas, and to-do lists onto a single sheet of paper. This simple habit, a "morning mind dump," helps me clear mental clutter, allowing me to focus on the tasks ahead with clarity and purpose. As I scribble down random thoughts, something magical happens – priorities emerge, and my day takes shape.

**Prioritizing with a Penny**
I've found that carrying a single penny in my pocket serves as a tangible reminder to prioritize my most important tasks of the day. Whenever I'm feeling overwhelmed, I take a moment to pull out the penny and flip it, allowing myself to refocus on what truly needs attention. This humble habit helps me cut through distractions and stay on track, one penny at a time.

**The Power of 90**
Each day, I allocate 90 minutes to my most critical task, without any breaks
