Meet `strace`, the command-line detective that unearths hidden secrets beneath the surface of your Linux system. Like a digital Sherlock Holmes, `strace` inspects the calls made by any process in real-time, revealing a treasure trove of information about system calls, file operations, and network interactions.

With a single command, you can transform into a master of system exploration: `strace -p <PID>`. This allows you to scrutinize the system calls made by a process with a process ID of `<PID>`, gaining an intimate understanding of the system's intricacies and exposing performance bottlenecks. By wielding `strace`, you wield unimaginable power to diagnose, debug, and optimize your Linux systems.
